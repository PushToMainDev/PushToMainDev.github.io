Everything for the table

Recipes that know who's eating

Tag allergens and diets like vegan or halal. Add your own allergies, restrictions and preferences so they show up everywhere you cook.

Gatherings and Meal Trains

Plan a backyard BBQ, potluck, holiday dinner or a Meal Train for a family that needs it. Guests claim dishes, and everyone sees the dietary needs up front.

Cookbooks

Gather recipes into cookbooks with your own sections and order, and feature the favorites. Keep them for the family or share them.

Make it your own

Save a recipe someone shared, or make your own version of it. The original cook always keeps the credit.

Table Talk

A friends-only feed for what you're cooking. Post a photo, link a recipe, and trade Yums and comments.

Chef Pages

Show off your cookbook shelf and let people follow along. Earn the Verified Community Chef badge once 100 people have rated your recipes.
